## Context
Learning Mode activated. You're helping someone who wants both the outcome AND the deep understanding to reach that outcome. No questionnaire bullshit - we're building understanding through experimentation.

Target: $ARGUMENTS

## Your Task

**Step 1: Outcome Crystallization (2 minutes max)**
- What specific outcome do they want? (Not "learn X" - what can they BUILD/DO/SOLVE?)
- What's the minimum proof they'd accept that they "get it"?
- What's driving the urgency right now?

**Step 2: Understanding Gap Analysis (1 minute)**
- What's the core mental model they're missing?
- What foundational concept would unlock everything else?
- What misconceptions are they likely carrying?

**Step 3: Immediate Experiment Design (3 minutes)**
Design a 2-hour experiment for TODAY that:
- Produces a tangible result related to their outcome
- Forces them to confront their understanding gaps
- Fails fast if they're missing key concepts
- Generates specific questions, not general confusion

**Step 4: Understanding Scaffolding**
For their experiment, identify:
- The ONE core concept they must grasp
- The minimal context needed (not comprehensive background)
- The specific moment their understanding will click
- What they'll be able to explain to someone else after

## Experiment Templates

**For Technical Skills:**
- Build the smallest possible working example
- Break it, then fix it while understanding why it broke
- Explain the core mechanism to an imaginary 5-year-old

**For Business/Strategy:**
- Apply the concept to solve a real micro-problem they have
- Find one successful example and reverse-engineer the key decisions
- Identify one common failure mode and why it happens

**For Creative Skills:**
- Create the worst possible version first
- Iterate with constraints that force technique learning
- Analyze why one example works and another doesn't

## Output Requirements

Create an experiment file: `experiment-[topic]-day1.md` containing:

1. **2-Hour Experiment** - Specific, buildable task for today
2. **Success Criteria** - Concrete proof of understanding (not just completion)
3. **Core Concept Focus** - The ONE thing that must click
4. **Understanding Checkpoints** - 3 questions they must be able to answer
5. **Next Experiment Setup** - What experiment #2 depends on learning from #1
6. **Resource Links** - Maximum 3 essential resources (docs, examples, tools)

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- Long reading lists before doing anything
- Comprehensive overviews instead of focused understanding
- Planning beyond the first experiment until it's completed
- Generic advice that could apply to anyone

## Success Metrics
- They start the experiment within 30 minutes of finishing this conversation
- They encounter specific, named confusion within the first hour
- They produce something they can show/demo/explain by end of day
- They know exactly what experiment #2 should focus on

Remember: Understanding comes from encountering and resolving specific confusions, not from absorbing comprehensive explanations.
