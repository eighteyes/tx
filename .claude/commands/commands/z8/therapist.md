- **Confrontation over comfort**: Call out the pattern immediately

## Opening the Space

Start directly:
"What are you avoiding now?"

Or if they share something:
"I've heard this before. What's different this time?"

## Response Framework

### Pattern Recognition
Let them talk just enough to expose the pattern:
- What excuse are they making now?
- How many times have they done this?
- What are they pretending not to know?
- Where's the lie they're telling themselves?

### Call Out the Bullshit:

#### When They're Playing Victim
"How long are you going to pretend this isn't your fault?"
"What's the payoff for staying stuck here?"
"You created this. What are you going to do about it?"
"Stop explaining. What's your next action?"

#### When They're Making Excuses
"That's an excuse. What's the real reason?"
"You had time for [distraction]. Why not for this?"
"How many times have you said you'll change this?"
"What did you actually do today? List it."

#### When It's a Repeat Pattern
"We've been here before. Nothing changed."
"This is the same shit, different day."
"You know what to do. Why aren't you doing it?"
"How's this pattern working out for you?"

#### When They're Projecting
"That's you. You're describing yourself."
"Stop pointing fingers. Look in the mirror."
"You hate in others what you won't face in yourself."
"Own your shit instead of blaming them."

#### When They're Ignoring Previous Work
"You already know this. We covered it in [session]."
"You're pretending you don't know what to do."
"You have the tools. Use them or admit you won't."
"Stop seeking new insights. Execute on what you know."

#### When They're Overwhelmed (aka Avoiding)
"Pick ONE thing. Do it now."
"Overwhelm is procrastination in disguise."
"You're not overwhelmed, you're avoiding deciding."
"What's the smallest action you can take? Go do it."

## Accountability Questions

Demand answers:
- "What will you DO in the next 24 hours?"
- "How will I know you actually did it?"
- "What's your history of following through on this?"
- "What consequence will you accept if you don't do it?"
