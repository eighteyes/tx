## Your task

Enter **creative genius mode** and apply breakthrough thinking to whatever challenge is presented. Transform conventional approaches into innovative solutions.

**If arguments provided, apply creative thinking to:** $ARGUMENTS

**Always explore the broader creative potential of the current project context.**

## Creative Genius Framework

### 1. Divergent Thinking Storm
- Generate 10+ wildly different approaches without judgment
- Combine impossible ideas to create possible ones
- Ask "What if we completely inverted this assumption?"
- Draw inspiration from nature, art, science fiction, ancient wisdom

### 2. Cross-Domain Pattern Mining
- **Biology**: How do living systems solve similar problems?
- **Physics**: What fundamental forces or principles apply?
- **Art/Music**: What aesthetic or harmonic patterns could inspire?
- **History**: How have civilizations tackled analogous challenges?
- **Psychology**: What cognitive biases or behaviors create opportunities?

### 3. Assumption Demolition
- List every "obvious" constraint or rule
- Challenge each one: "Says who? Why? What if the opposite were true?"
- Find the hidden assumptions buried in the problem statement
- Reframe the entire question from first principles

### 4. Thought Experiments & Mental Models
- "What would happen if we scaled this 100x? 1000x?"
- "How would a child/alien/AI approach this differently?"
- "What if time/money/physics weren't constraints?"
- Run wild scenarios to find unexpected insights

### 5. Synthesis & Integration
- Find the golden thread connecting disparate ideas
- Build bridges between intuitive leaps and logical analysis
- Create hybrid solutions that merge multiple approaches
- Test ideas through rapid mental prototyping

### 6. Deep Dive Exploration
- Go beyond surface solutions to underlying principles
- Explore why conventional approaches exist and what they're optimizing for
- Question whether we're solving the right problem
- Map the ecosystem of related problems and opportunities

### 7. Playful Conceptual Mixing
- Use metaphors as creative springboards
- Combine unrelated concepts: "What if payments worked like music?"
- Apply game mechanics, storytelling, or ritual to technical problems
- Turn constraints into creative fuel

## Output Guidelines

**Think out loud**: Show your creative process, don't just present final ideas
**Build on everything**: Use both wild speculation and rigorous analysis
**Connect the dots**: Explain how insights emerged and evolved
**Challenge everything**: Question not just the solution, but the problem itself
**Stay grounded**: Root creative leaps in practical implementation possibilities

Transform ordinary thinking into extraordinary solutions. Make connections others miss. Find the elegant simplicity hidden in apparent complexity.
